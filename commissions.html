Terms

By commissioning work, you agree to the terms here.

Payment
  • 50% deposit required before work begins. The remaining 50% is due on completion, before final files are delivered.
  • Deposits are non-refundable once work has started.
  • Full refunds are only available if you cancel before any work begins.
Scope and revisions
  • Project scope is agreed on before starting. Requests outside the original scope may cost extra.
  • 2 free revisions are included per project. Further revisions are billed separately.
Priority
  • Priority costs an extra 30% on top of the quoted price, in USD or Robux.
  • Your project moves to the front of the waitlist and starts in the next open slot, ahead of standard requests.
  • Projects already in progress are not paused or bumped for priority work.
  • If more than one client has priority, they're served first come, first served.
  • The priority fee is paid up front together with the 50% deposit.
  • The priority fee is non-refundable once your project has moved ahead, unless the start is delayed more than 3 days past the priority estimate because of me. In that case the fee is refunded.
  • Late feedback or replies from you can still push back the delivery date.
Timeline
  • You'll get an estimated delivery time at the start of each project.
  • Late feedback or replies from you may push back the final delivery date.
Ownership and credit
  • Final files are yours to use once payment is complete.
  • Credit is appreciated but not required, unless we agree otherwise.
  • I keep the right to show completed work in my portfolio, unless we discuss otherwise.
Conduct
  • I can decline or cancel a project at my discretion, with a refund of any unused portion of payment.
  • Disrespectful or abusive behavior toward me ends the project immediately, with no refund.
